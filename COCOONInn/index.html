Capsule stays that move with your city

COCOONInn is a portable hotel chain built with modular, container-based structures. Designed for short-term guests at railway stations, airports, and other transient hubs, it provides comfortable, private stays where permanent buildings aren't practical or sustainable.

Portable Hotel Units

About COCOONInn

Instead of building heavy, immovable hotels, COCOONInn runs on modular containers that host compact, comfortable sleeping pods. This lets us operate in high-demand, high-rent pockets – from dense city centers to transit hubs – without burning capital on permanent structures.

Our franchise partners plug into a ready-made format: optimized layouts, shared amenities, and a tech-first booking and access layer. Guests get privacy, essentials, and smart pricing. Operators get higher yield per square foot and the freedom to move where the demand is.

Core Concept

Container-based hotel-chain: Each COCOONInn unit is built inside standardized containers that can be stacked, clustered, and transported as needed.

Capsule-first design: Vertically stacked pods, shared utilities, and smart circulation maximize guest capacity in minimal space.

The Problem

High accommodation cost. Long, uncomfortable waits for delayed transport. Paying for a full day when you only need a few hours. These are everyday realities for travelers worldwide.

High Cost
Space Crunch
Short Stays

01 · High accommodation costs

Prime-location hotels carry huge land and construction costs, which flow directly into room prices. Short-stay and budget-conscious travelers are priced out.

02 · Space crunch drives up rates

Traditional hotel rooms are horizontal and spacious, resulting in fewer guests per floor and forcing operators to push tariffs higher.

03 · All-or-nothing pricing

During delays or layovers, travelers only need a clean bed, storage, Wi‑Fi, and a shower. Instead, they are pushed into full-day bookings.

The COCOONInn Solution

COCOONInn uses capsule pods and container-based structures to create a new category of truly flexible hotels – designed for short, efficient stays instead of full-day lock-ins.

Cost-effective capsule hotels

Capsules focus on essentials: bed, storage, ventilation, lighting, privacy, power. By eliminating wasted space, COCOONInn can offer stays at much lower prices than conventional hotels.

Space-optimized layouts

Vertically stacked pods and shared facilities increase guests per container. More revenue per footprint – without needing to build large permanent structures.

Portability & Flexibility

COCOONInn is built as a container-based hotel-chain. Each property is made of modular containers housing pods, utilities, and service zones.

If demand shifts, regulations change, or a better transit hub opens, the entire hotel can be moved. No demolition, no dead real estate – just redeployment.

That portability gives operators and franchise partners a significant hedge against location risk.

Product & Usage

COCOONInn is optimized for short-term, high-rotation stays – powered by a tech layer that keeps operations lean and transparent.

Guest Journey

Guests arrive at a transit node or city pocket, book a pod for 3–24 hours, access via digital credentials, rest, shower, charge devices, and leave – without paying for unused hours.

Profitability

Higher pod density and short-stay rotation mean more revenue per container. This asset-light model enables faster payback for franchise partners.

Tech Layer

Time-based pricing, pod allocation logic, digital keys, and dashboards for occupancy and revenue – all built to keep operations predictable and scalable.
